Our successful projects

Our cases demonstrate the most successful examples of bilateral cooperation. Here are specific projects that reflect our joint work with Irish and Ukrainian partners, as well as their impact on the economic and cultural development of both countries.

Case 1: Establishing cooperation between the Lviv Chamber of Commerce and the Cork Chamber of Commerce

Client: Lviv Chamber of Commerce and Industry and the Cork Chamber of Commerce.

Objective: To facilitate business contacts between Ukrainian and Irish enterprises, the exchange of experience and the development of bilateral trade relations.

Solution by Hibernian Group:

  1. Organizing an online meeting between representatives of the Lviv CCI, Cork CCI, the Women's Business Chamber of Ukraine and other stakeholders.
  2. Moderating a discussion on the business climate in Ukraine, operating in wartime conditions and measures to support entrepreneurs.
  3. Presenting the "Hibernian Group" as a platform for developing Ukrainian-Irish relations and inviting cooperation.
  4. Initiating the development of a cooperation roadmap involving the Lviv CCI, Cork CCI, "Hibernian Group", the Embassy of Ukraine in Ireland and the Embassy of Ireland in Ukraine.
Result: Direct contact was established between the Lviv CCI and the Cork CCI. Agreements were reached on further cooperation and joint initiatives. Hibernian Group received support from both chambers and expanded its network of contacts.
