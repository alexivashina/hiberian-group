Bridging Ireland and Ukraine through comprehensive collaboration

Hibernian Group is a platform that brings together business, education, and culture to foster effective partnerships and international cooperation between Ireland and Ukraine. We also promote cultural exchange, the development of education, and provide humanitarian aid to those in need.

We believe in the power of human unity and strive to make the world a better place to live.

Our Mission

Our goal is to establish sustainable and long-lasting relationships between Ukraine and Ireland, based on mutual respect, cooperation, and cultural exchange. We aim to strengthen cultural, economic, and humanitarian ties, fostering the development of both nations through educational, business, and social initiatives. By supporting innovative projects, we provide a platform for building partnerships that unlock new opportunities for collaboration. Our Ukraine-Ireland team is focused on long-term success, ensuring sustainable growth and mutual benefit for both countries.

Services for Irish Companies

Hibernian Group provides comprehensive support for Irish companies looking to enter the Ukrainian market or establish partnerships with Ukrainian counterparts. We offer a full range of business services, including legal advice, adaptation to local economic conditions, and cultural considerations.

  • Legal consultations and ensuring compliance with local laws
  • Support in the establishment of joint ventures in Ukraine
  • Evaluation of investment and partnership potential
  • Audit of potential partners to assess reliability
  • Preparation and accompaniment of business delegations to Ukraine
  • Organization of business forums and meetings

Stay updated

Stay updated with the latest news from Hibernian Group and don't miss out on important events! We regularly host business forums, cultural activities, and conferences aimed at strengthening Ukrainian-Irish cooperation.

20/03/2025

Meeting of the Hibernian Group with Enterprise Ireland Poland & Baltics

On March 20, a meeting was held with Bartosz Siepracki, Senior Market Adviser for Digital Tech & High-Tech Construction at Enterprise Ireland Poland & Baltics. Key touchpoints and promising areas for collaboration were identified. In the near future, joint projects in education and business partnership development will be launched.

30/01/2025

Lviv CCI (Ukraine) and Cork CCI (Ireland) held an online meeting

On January 30, 2025, the parties discussed cooperation and investment opportunities. The "Hibernian Group" was introduced, which will focus on developing Ukrainian-Irish relations. The parties agreed to develop a roadmap for cooperation involving various stakeholders, including the embassies of both countries.
